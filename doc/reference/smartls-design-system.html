Overview

A corporate-blue B2B logistics site built on Bootstrap 5.3.3 with a single 98 KB custom stylesheet layered on top. The system is photo-led: nearly every section either sits on a photograph with a blue-to-black scrim, or on a near-white wash. Content lives in soft-shadowed 18px-radius cards. Orange is used sparingly and always as the interruption — eyebrows, icons, hover states, the active carousel dot.

Typography

Two Google faces, cleanly divided: Montserrat for anything structural (headings, buttons, labels, stat figures) and Manrope for everything read as prose (body, nav links, form fields). Weight is the primary expressive tool — 800 and 900 appear 86 times in the stylesheet against 8 uses of 400.

Fluid sizing appears only on the hero rule: clamp(2.05rem, 1.55rem + 2.1vw, 2.95rem). Everything else steps at breakpoints or not at all. Sizes are authored in irregular rem values (.78, .92, 1.02, 1.05) rather than on a ratio — there is no formal scale to inherit, only conventions.

Section band recipe

  • White + wash — .section-bg--wash, a 1200×620 radial of rgba(31,153,216,.10) at 12% 10%.
  • Platinum surface — .section-bg--surface, #F4F6F7 at 65–72%.
  • Fixed photo — .section-bg--fixed, parallax background image under the blue→black scrim. Used for Industries.
  • Charcoal — footer and testimonial card only.

Forms

  • Bootstrap .form-control, largely unstyled: 6px radius, white fill, ~57px tall with 16px padding, 16.8px text.
  • Labels are Manrope 700, sentence case, sitting directly above the field.
  • Focus uses a brand-recolored ring, 0 0 0 .25rem rgba(31,153,216,.12).
  • File input is the raw browser control — the only unstyled element on the site.
  • Submit buttons are full-width on mobile, auto-width on desktop.

Navigation

  • Desktop — fixed white bar, 84px, logo left, five Manrope 700 links (#1F99D8), then an EN language pill and a solid blue GET A QUOTE button.
  • Mobile — hamburger collapses into a translucent dropdown panel; active item sits in a pale pill with blue text, remaining items in charcoal; language pill and CTA stack beneath.
  • Toggler swaps to an X glyph when open.

Carousel & stepper

  • Testimonials — charcoal card, circular blue initials avatar, name in Montserrat 700 with role beneath in muted grey, curly quotes on the body. Controls are dark circular chevrons; indicators are small squares, blue for inactive and rgba(238,125,4,.85) for active.
  • Quote wizard — four numbered circular steps with uppercase Montserrat labels (NEED / ROUTE / DETAILS / CONTACT), active step filled blue, plus a thin orange→blue progress bar across the card top. Options are selectable cards with a centered icon chip; the selected card takes a blue border and tinted fill.

Responsive behaviour

Breakpoints are Bootstrap's, with custom rules concentrated at 991.98px (nav collapse) and 575.98px (type and padding compression).

BreakpointWhat changes
≥992pxFull horizontal nav; multi-column rows; p-lg-5 raises card padding from 24px to 48px
≤991.98pxNavbar collapses to hamburger (measured 68px tall at 375px, min-height rule 56px); nav links shrink to .95rem; all rows stack to single column
≤767pxCard grids fully stacked; media tiles go full-width
≤575.98pxHero type steps down via clamp() — H1 56→30.4px, section titles 32→24.6px; buttons go full-width. Section padding stays 80px, so vertical rhythm is not compressed on phones
hover: noneTransform-based hover lifts are disabled on touch
prefers-reduced-motionReveal animations and transitions suppressed

Observed at 375px

  • Header stays fixed and white; content clears it correctly on every page.
  • Home hero keeps the full-viewport photo; the track field and TRACK button sit side by side rather than stacking, which keeps the input narrow.
  • Every card grid becomes one column with 16px gaps; card padding stays 24px.
  • Stat cards, industry cards, service pillars and article cards all reflow cleanly — no overflow, no horizontal scroll anywhere.
  • The Services anchor pills wrap to three rows and stay tappable.
  • Footer stacks brand block, then Quick Links, Corporate Policies, Contact.

Motion

  • Scroll reveal — [data-reveal] elements start at opacity:0; translateY(18px) and transition over .6s ease to visible, toggled by an .is-visible class.
  • Card hover — transform .18s ease, box-shadow .18s ease, the single most repeated transition (8 uses).
  • Button hover — background and border flip to orange over .18s, with a lift and a blue-tinted shadow.
  • Counters — stat figures animate up from 0 when the section enters the viewport. A page scraped before the trigger fires reads 0+.
  • Header — transition: height .2s ease, with a cubic-bezier(.2,.9,.2,1) transform on the mobile panel.
  • Marquee — client logos scroll continuously in the band under the hero.

Findings

Things worth fixing, found while walking the pages at both widths.

Mobile menu panel is too transparent

The collapsed nav uses a translucent fill, so the hero photograph and its headline read straight through the menu items. Charcoal links over a moving photo is the weakest contrast on the site.

375px · every page · .navbar-collapse.show

Carousel arrows sit on top of the quote text

At 375px the previous/next chevrons overlap the left and right edges of the testimonial card, clipping the first and last characters of several lines.

375px · home · .testimonials-pro__control

Body copy over busy photographs

Several service and industry cards place white Manrope 400 over photographs where the scrim thins out — legible in places, marginal in others. A minimum scrim opacity, or a solid plate behind the text, would make it consistent.

All widths · services, industries

Input radius contradicts the system

Every card is 18px and every button 12px, but form fields keep Bootstrap's 6px default. On the partner intake and quote wizard — the two places conversion happens — the inputs read as un-designed next to their container.

All widths · .form-control

Button padding is set per instance

.btn-smart appears with 8/16, 16/24 and 6/12 padding depending on which Bootstrap utilities were attached. Three named sizes on the class itself would remove the drift.

All widths · site-wide

Two page backgrounds hotlink Wikimedia

Smart Track and Smart Quote both pull their world-map background from upload.wikimedia.org. That is a third-party dependency on two conversion pages, and Wikimedia's policy discourages hotlinking. Self-host the asset.

All widths · smart-track, smart-quote

Two section rhythms coexist

Home sections use 80px vertical padding; Services and Kaizen use 76px. The difference is invisible in isolation and untraceable in maintenance — pick one and token it.

≥992px · .section vs .service-page__section

No dark theme

No prefers-color-scheme rules exist. Given the palette already carries a charcoal ground (footer, testimonial card), a dark mode would be a small extension rather than a rebuild.
